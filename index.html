RESEARCH NOTES

Methodology.

What made the cut. We could price 50 public calls across 24 dates. Three scored dates came from X and 21 from YouTube. The wider inventory covered 855 video titles and descriptions, with 253 further candidates awaiting verification. This is a partial sample.

How the cherry picks work. For each of 50 calls, we kept its largest gross inverse return across completed 30-day, 90-day and one-year windows. We ranked those results and kept the top three calls. Selecting a pick opens its winning window. Each return belongs to one hypothetical trade.

What a reversal means. A bullish call gets a synthetic short. A sale, trim or avoid gets a long. The follow strategy buys bullish calls and keeps the sold or avoided slice in cash at 0%. Some statements describe holdings or allocation changes.

When the clock starts. Entry uses the first available close after the publication day. Exit uses the first close on or after 30, 90 or 365 calendar days from entry. Only completed windows enter the results.

How we compare. Each publication date receives ₹10,000, split equally among its calls. We average the date-level returns. Foreign positions include USD/INR moves. Nifty BeES supplies the matched benchmark. Each chart uses adjusted closes or fund NAVs, and the last available benchmark or FX mark when markets close on different days.

What the figures omit. Fees, spreads, financing, borrow availability, margin calls and taxes are excluded. Research prices end on 20 November 2026. Direct property and REIT calls are outside this edition.
